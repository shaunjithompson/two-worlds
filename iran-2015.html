The route

Tap a stop to jump to it

Dot map of Iran and its neighbours, with Iran's land borders. Tehran is marked as the capital. A faint line comes down from Ashgabat in Turkmenistan to Mashhad; the route runs west along the Alborz to Golestan, on to Tehran, then north-west to Tabriz, and a faint line continues to Jolfa on the Aras and into Armenia.

Mashhad

Map ↑
مشهدIran2–3 August 2015

Over the border from Ashgabat and down to Mashhad by bus; the driver poured me a glass of tea on the way. Mashhad grew up around the shrine of Imam Reza.

02

Golestan

Map ↑
گلستانIran5–7 August 2015

In Mashhad a hiking group from Golestan asked me to walk with them: three days from Aliabad-e Katul, through beech forest above the village of Mahian and over the ridge to the Semnan border, down to a waterfall.

Tehran

تهرانIran9–11 August 2015

Tehran, under the Alborz. The Grand Bazaar is sorted by trade; my host took me up to Meygun, a mountain village an hour north of the city.
