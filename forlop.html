The inspiration

In 1848 Adolph Tidemand and Hans Gude painted Bridal Procession on the Hardangerfjord: a wedding party crossing the fjord by boat, between steep slopes, with the church on the shore behind them.

Forløp is a slow, cold song in the manner of Grieg’s slow songs.
